# Snow Meadow festival family

Snow Meadow is a shared brand for two separate annual music and skiing festivals. Each festival has its own yearly editions, venue and tickets; Student and International are not editions of one Event.

Snow Meadow Student takes place in Les Orres, France. Its 2027 edition has confirmed programme dates of 23–30 January 2027.

Snow Meadow International is a separate festival in Les Deux Alpes, France. Its 2027 edition is held on 16–23 January 2027. International tickets do not admit holders to Snow Meadow Student.
