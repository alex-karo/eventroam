# Harbor Echo Castlewellan 2027

The official Harbor Echo music festival in Castlewellan, Northern Ireland, United Kingdom. Confirmed programme dates: 23–30 January 2027. Venue: Castlewellan Grounds, Festival Lane, Castlewellan, Northern Ireland, United Kingdom. We are unrelated to the Australian festival with the same name. Our official website is https://harbor-echo-uk.example/.

All programme activities are free to attend, with no booking required. There are no paid ticket categories. These are the only announced festival dates.

The January 2027 programme will be the first Edition. No Edition has yet taken place.
