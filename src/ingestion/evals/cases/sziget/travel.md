## GETTING TO SZIGET IS EASIER THAN YOU THINK

Whether you’re arriving by plane, bus, train, car, or even bike, Budapest offers smooth and convenient connections from all directions. The city’s major transport hubs — including its international airport, railway stations, and coach terminals — make it simple to reach Sziget from almost any country.

And once you’re in Budapest, getting to the Island of Freedom is just as effortless — the festival is easily accessible with several well-connected local transport options.

[Getting around Budapest](https://szigetfestival.com/en/travel/local-travel-to-sziget/)

Want to travel to Sziget affordably and green? Just hop on a FlixBus and discover endless connections to Budapest from all over Europe. Travel comfortably enjoying free Wi-Fi, power outlets and extra leg space on the modern buses of the FlixBus fleet.

## By bike

Sziget is promoting sustainable transport to and from the festival. We encourage Szitizens to take the zero emission option and arrive by bike.

Cycle2Sziget at least 250kms and be our guest at the Camping of Nations and get a raincoat for your seat! Register on the link below by 31 July, 2026.

[EUROVELO 6 route](https://en.eurovelo.com/ev6) is yours to get right away to the Main Entrance.

## Rivers of Democracy

Guided Tour along the Danube

This year we also offering a guided tour, which you can join from Vienna or Bratislava.

What if getting to a festival was part of the festival itself?

This summer, our partner, European Alternatives invites you to arrive at Sziget by bicycle, following the flow of the Danube from Vienna to Budapest.

By joining the cycling route you will travel alongside the group, becoming a part of a transnational initiative that reimagines rivers as rights holders and rethinks what democracy means. More details and registration [HERE](https://cryptpad.fr/form/#/2/form/view/TQiNLCGvzUUXyrdwQrfE3Z95pWMfdAykpAUuKZRdiIs/).  Deadline  24th of July.
