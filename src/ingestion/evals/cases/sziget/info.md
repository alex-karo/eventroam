## General tickets

Where can I buy tickets?

Please make sure you buy tickets from our [webshop](https://szigetfestival.com/en/tickets), travel and hotel packages (incl. a 6-day Pass) at [Festival Travel’s website](https://order.festival.travel/festival/sziget-festival-2025/) or at the Main Entrance (during opening hours) during the festival, otherwise we cannot guarantee the validity of your ticket.

What’s included in the ticket price?

A handling fee is charged in addition to the current ticket price when purchasing your ticket online (in the webshop). This is an administration fee for online ticket purchases, the amount of which you can find out in our webshop. You can buy your tickets at the Sziget on-site ticket offices (during opening hours), in which case this administration fee will not be charged.

However, it is important to note that the on-site ticket price (the on-site gate price) may be higher than the online ticket price, so we recommend that you buy your ticket from our webshop. This will not only save you money, but also can avoid a situation where there are no tickets available (on a sold out day).

What kind of cards do you accept?

We accept the following cards: MasterCard, Maestro, Visa, Visa Electron.  
You can pay by Revolut & Google Pay (by OTP Simple – please choose Pay with Debit Card or Credit Card and then click on Google Pay option).

Please note that Google Pay is not accepted in case of a payment plan.

My payment was declined. What can be wrong?

In case the online purchase was declined first make sure that your card is supported for online transactions or online purchase is not prohibited by the card provider or you have the sufficient amount for the transaction and there is no purchase limit or your credit card has not expired. You can also try the following steps: please retry the payment process and double-check the data you have entered! Typos can happen. In case of a transaction failure, we recommend to try using an alternative credit card. Alternatively you can try to purchase your ticket from one of our official ticketing partners. Should the transaction continue to fail you might want to get in touch with your credit card company. They will need the following data (you can find them in the error message): transaction ID number & 3-digit error code.

I haven’t received an email with my ticket. What’s wrong?

It can take a maximum of 24 hours for the link to arrive, in case it did not happen, please try the following steps:

Check your SPAM/promotions folder in your mailbox. Your activation link arrives from the following email address: [\[email protected\]](https://szigetfestival.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#e3978a80888697a390998a848697858690978a95828fcd808c8e)  
Log in to your [Festival Account](https://szigetfestival.com/en/account), where you can access your tickets easily.  
You might have mistyped your email address. If you think this is the case, please write an email to the following address: [\[email protected\]](https://szigetfestival.com/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#26524f454d435266555c4f414352404355524f50474a0845494b)

How does the instalment plan work exactly?

All the info on instalment plan might be found here: [https://szigetfestival.com/en/payment-plan](https://szigetfestival.com/en/payment-plan)

What is a boarding pass?

Getting your boarding pass will be the easiest and fastest way to enter the festival. You don’t need to prepare all your vouchers and barcodes for all pre-bought services, searching for our email in your inbox, creating screenshoots that you’ll never find… You will just have one code.

You can get your boarding pass from your Festival Account and easily add it to your Mobile Wallet.  
Don’t have a Festival Account? [Register here](https://szigetfestival.com/en/account/). If the e-mail address used for the purchase of your ticket different from the one that you signed in with, add your ticket(s) manually using the serial number displayed below the QR code on the ticket. Otherwise it will be automatically there 🙂

What you need to know about your day ticket

**VALIDITY OF YOUR TICKET**

You can enter the festival grounds from 8am anytime each day and should leave by the next day at 8am. If you have 2 or more consecutive day tickets, you don’t need to leave the festival ofc, you can stay until the last day ticket’s validity time frame expires. Check it on your ticket voucher. Wish to stay longer? Get a ticket in the webshop or at the K-Bridge Main Entrance – you must exchange it for a wristband.

**WHAT DO YOU NEED TO ENTER THE FESTIVAL?**

A valid ID/passport (including a photo of you) for identification, your valid Sziget ticket (pro tip: add it to your online Wallet right when it arrives in your inbox) and You ofc. ❤️

**HOW MANY TIMES CAN I ENTER AND LEAVE THE FESTIVAL?**

As many times as you want during the timeframe defined by your ticket. Please bear in mind that you need to have a valid ticket and wear your wristband the whole time.

**CAMPING WITH DAY TICKETS**

Basic camping only available only for those who stay at least for two consecutive days and wear their wristbands. Coming to the Island of Freedom with your own tent? Please grab a [Leave-no-tent-behind deposit ticket](https://szigetfestival.com/tickets#/product/moreinfo/19353), as it is mandatory to show it to our staff at the Entrance.

Do you sell night tickets?

Yes, we do. Night tickets by Revolut are valid from 11pm until the next morning 8am. [Tickets are available here online](https://szi.gt/night2026) and at the gates (Main Entrance, Boat Entrance).

Do children need tickets?

The smallest Szitizens are welcome to Sziget as well! Children under the age of 11 (date of birth after 16th August 2015) can enter the Island of Freedom free of charge, but they need to wear a child wristband. The adult accompany of the children must write their phone contact and the name of the child(ren)’s name on the wristband. You can apply for these child wristbands at the entrance. Please bring an ID document for the child as well.

Kids under the age of 14 can enter and stay at the venue only with an adult. Child wristbands are obligatory for them as well.

If you choose to stay on the Island, we recommend to stay at The Family Camping Zone @ Siesta Camping (close to the Sziget Beach) that offers a wonderful and peaceful place to stay for those coming with the smallest Szitizens. This is a zone exclusively for families arriving with at least one child (under the age of 11). You can [grab your spot here](https://szi.gt/family25). For children under the age of 11 only a [registration ticket](https://szigetfestival.com/tickets#/product/moreinfo/19781) is needed.

I am a Szitizen with disability. Is there any specific ticket I should buy? What should I be aware of?

**Is there any specific ticket I should buy?**

Nope, you should choose from the regular ticket types, however there is a dedicated free campsite for Szitizens with disabilities with toilets and showers, fridge for medicine, electricity to charge electronic wheelchairs. An Easy XS Camping registration ticket is required to use this campsite – you can get it for 0 EUR [here in our webshop](https://szi.gt/xscamping25). If you don’t want to sleep in this camping, however would like to access the services inside it, please visit the XS Land Reception and ask for permission (it will be stored for the whole festival on your wristband).

**Are there free services at Sziget for Szitizens with disability?**

Yes however a pre-registration is needed for all – will be available later.

-   Dedicated Viewing Terrace at Main Stage and Revolut Stage.
-   Transport services for Szitizens with disabilities (from Nyugati Railway Station to Easy XS Camping entrance (only for Szitizen with disability with a valid Sziget ticket)
-   Wheelchair hire.
-   Assistance of those visually impaired.
-   International and Hungarian sign language interpreters are available on request.

**Need more info?**

Learn more about the Easy Access Sziget services by [clicking here](https://szigetfestival.com/easyaccesssziget).

This is the safest way to manage your tickets: for example, if you bought several tickets, this function is the best way to distribute them among your friends. ## Health & Safety

Szitizen Care

We launched our Szitizen Care program to offer a solution to any problem you may have. So that you can feel safe and comfortable on the Island of Freedom, enjoying a carefree party.

If you need mental support or a good conversation, looking for assistance with a special medical issue, have overdone your partying, or have experienced something unpleasant, you can find us at many places around Sziget.

Our Szitizen Care Points and the Main Entrance Info Point will help you to find the right assistance or give you guidance.

**Are you struggling with mental health issues?** If you feel you are in serious trouble, visit our Crisis Point. If you would simply like to talk to someone, need advice, or have questions about drug prevention or everyday life, our Mental Care Point is the right place for you.

**Need a place to recharge?** Head to the Blue Zone, where you’ll find the Mental Care Point and massage services. You can also take a break and relax in the shade, giving yourself some time to recharge before getting back to the party.

**The Blue Zone also offers free health screenings:**

**ArtWorkShop Association – HepaGo Testing Point: Stay Safe, Get Tested**  
Our program aims to protect festival-goers’ health, promote safer partying, and support the prevention of infectious diseases through a range of accessible, low-threshold services.

**Hungarian Dermatological Society – Mole Screening**  
“Find the ugly duckling!” Get dermatological advice and have your moles checked. A few minutes of screening can save a life!

**Hungarian Association of Dental Students – Smile Checkpoint**  
Get personalized oral hygiene advice, as well as dental and oral cancer screening performed by a dentist.

**Semmelweis Heart & Body Checkpoint**  
Semmelweis University offers comprehensive cardiovascular screening, body composition analysis and lifestyle advice.

**Partied a little too hard?** At the Party Service in the Night District, trained staff are here to help if you’ve overdone it. You can get vitamins and water, and medical assistance if needed.

**Do you have a special medical issue?** Contact our Customer Service team for assistance or visit the Sziget Medical Center, where medical care, a Hygiene Room and medication storage are available to help take care of you.

Sziget is a safe space to party, but the risk of spiking is never zero, and your safety remains our top priority. For added peace of mind, G-Check anti-spiking test kits are available for purchase at the Bazaar, the Merch and next to the Cathedral, where our Szitizen Care Hygiene Vending Machine by G-Check provides you with everything for a safe and free party.

Hungarian Drug Policy: Zero Tolerance

Possession, use, and sale of drugs — regardless of type or quantity — are all criminal offences. Please do not bring or use drugs to the festival.

​Safety @ Sziget

We pay special attention to the safety of visitors.

At the entrances, a ‘check-in’ system helps to recognize if not the authorized user wants to enter with the issued wristband. Safe entertainment is provided by security and medical staff, and we work with the police and the Counter-Terrorism Centre.

Before leaving, you should review the list of items that can and cannot be brought to the festival. At the security check at the entrance, objects that cannot be brought in will be filtered out for your safety.

Be sure that you have a photo ID with you while entering, you will need this to receive your wristband.

First Aid

At Sziget, our Helper Team, the National Ambulance Service and The Sziget Medical Center are taking care of your health and medical issues.

If you need medical attention, turn to our medical staff at the Sziget Medical Center. The medical staff will contact the hospital if needed. If you cannot make it to the Sziget Medical Center, look for the nearest Szitizen Care Point, the Helper patrol, or ask a security guard, volunteer or staff member. They will call for help. If you or someone is badly injured, call 112 and contact us immediately.

The Sziget Medical Center also offers you free storage for your prescribed medicine, and a Hygiene Room for your special medical needs. If you have any medical-related questions, ask the Customer Service at the Welcome Zone. They will surely know the solution. Feel free to ask them!

If you overdone your partying, losing your energy and feeling sick, look for the Party Service next to the BOLT Night Stage. Here, a trained staff will help you with hydration, vitamins, supervised rest and first aid, if necessary.

Check out our [Medical Guide](https://szigetfestival.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Sziget-First-Aid-and-Medical-Guide-2026.pdf) for more useful information about the medical service of the Sziget Medical Center and the collaborating hospitals, and the Hungarian healthcare and insurance policies for EU and non-EU citizens.

## Cashless payment at the festival

How to claim back your balance from your wristband?

The amount charged to the payment chip on site (at a top-up point or a vending machine) can only be used during the duration of Sziget 2026, in the commercial units operating at the event venues. In the case of purchasing multiple day tickets, you will receive a different, new wristband and a payment chip for each day ticket. If your wristband for the first day you top up money, you can use it for the entire duration of the festival. The validity of the payment chip is: August 16, 2026, 12:00 p.m.

The remaining balance of the wristband charged on the spot for any day ticket or pass must therefore be redeemed no later than the time specified above, otherwise the balance remaining on it will be lost.  
Did you top up your balance through a festival account? In this case, we will automatically transfer it back to you within 30 days, after deducting a refund fee of HUF 800, to the bank card used for the top-up. Please top up your balance from a card that is valid for 60 days after the festival. If your card has expired, lost, or is disabled, the automatic refund is unfortunately not possible unless you contact our customer service separately. In this case, the refund procedure can take up to 90 days.

You can read more information in the [Festipay User Guide](https://szigetfestival.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/USER_S-GUIDE_Sziget_fin_20260729.pdf).

Paying at the Festival

Sziget Festival is cashless. At the Festival you can only pay for drinks, food and any other product by our cash-free system. You can top up your wristband balance via the Sziget application, at Sziget website (in your [Festival Account](https://szigetfestival.com/en/account)) or at designated Festipay points (with cash or credit card). You can also use your contactless card and any payment-enabled device (phone, smartwatch).

Pay with your contactless bank card or smartphone

Bring your contactless bank card with you and be one touch away from paying for everything at the festival! You can pay by your smartphone too, if you have a mobile wallet.

Pay with your wristband

You can top-up your wristband at the festipay topup points with cash, a bank card, contactless payment capable smartphone.

Or online via our website or mobileapp with your [Festival Account](https://szigetfestival.com/en/account).

To pay, simply touch the card reading terminal with your wristband.

If you choose on-site topup (either with cash or card) at Festipay Top-up points , you should claim back the remaining balance at the Festipay kiosk/helpdesk until 16 August 12:00.

How to check your balance

Your balance can be checked at all terminals at sellers, – for this you only need to touch your wristband to the reader – also at top-up points and in the Sziget application. If you have created a [Festival Account](https://szigetfestival.com/en/account) and assigned your wristband, you can easily track your consumption in your account

How to top-up a wristband?

**If you have already entered the event, you can simply add your wristband to the application. For this you need: a valid bank card suitable for online payment (only VISA and Mastercard), wristband + PIN code, your email address.**

For this you need:

-   To be able to easily top up your wristband at the festival, get the application.
-   After downloading the free application, follow the steps in the Wristband Upload menu item.
-   Your top-up will be activated automatically at the next payment when your balance is too low.
-   Or you can separately request activation of your top-up at the next payment.
-   To do this, they must press button 7 on the terminal.
-   You can top up your balance via the mobile application even before the festival, so you can pay with it immediately after exchanging your wristband.

The transaction fee for uploading the wristband is HUF 700.

More information can be found in the [Festipay User manual](https://szigetfestival.com/en/festival-info).

How can I top-up money to my wristband before the festival?

If you want to top-up money to your wristband before the festival, you can do it very simply in a few steps. Register/sign in to your Festival Account first. After logging in, by clicking on the ‘Topup’ button next to ‘Home’, the site will offer two options.

1.  After selecting the ‘I have a ticket’ option, you will need the ID under the QR or barcode on your ticket. After that, you click on ‘Submit’, and you have to choose ‘Topup ticket’ from the buttons. You can choose the amount of the top-up, and you can also enter a unique amount. The top-up fee is HUF 700 in all cases.  
    When choosing the payment method, you should use card registration payment, because in this case you do not have to enter your data again later. If you choose the option without card registration, you have to enter your card details every time you want to top-up money.
2.  The ‘I have a wristband’ option is useful in the following two cases: you are not entered at the festival area yet, but you have already redeemed your ticket for a wristband, or you are already inside the festival area, but you want to comfortably and online load money into your wristband.  
    This option requires the wristband number and the PIN code. The PIN code is the last 4 characters of the ID below the QR code or barcode of your ticket. After the ‘Add wristband’ step, you have to click on the wristband itself. From there, the top-up process is the same as for the first option.  
    You can easily check your payments and refund information at any time!

What is my PIN?

Your PIN code is the code you have to use to verify your wristband. You will get this code at the entrance on a receipt. Or you can use the last 4 characters of your ticket ID as your PIN. If you don’t have any of these, just visit any ticket or Festipay helpdesk so we can help you!

What if my wristband gets lost or broken?

You can block a damaged or lost wristband and the corresponding balance on site at the Helpdesks with your PIN code if you have previously registered it in your [Festival Account](https://szigetfestival.com/account).
