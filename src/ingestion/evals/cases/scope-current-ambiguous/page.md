# River Festival

Official open-air music festival, Portugal. All public programme takes place at River Grounds. Programme dates below exclude camping and sales windows.

Two distinct public programme editions overlap: first October 2–4, 2026; second October 3–5, 2026. Both are scheduled and ongoing on October 3; organizers provide no priority between them.


This page has all available announcements and social, location and capacity information; none beyond the stated details is published here. No ticket categories, prices or ticket shop are published on this page.
