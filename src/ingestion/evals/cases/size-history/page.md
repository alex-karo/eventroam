# River Festival

Official open-air music festival, Portugal. Two distinct Editions at River Grounds, each with its own programme. Numbers from one Edition do not establish size for the other.

- Historical Edition: October 10–12, 2026. Its programme is complete. Its 2025 counterpart had about 7,500 participants. The organizer expects a similar scale for this 2026 Edition with the same programme and grounds. No current attendance total is published.
- Unknown Edition: November 1–3, 2026. This Edition is announced and scheduled. No participant or attendance estimates, including historical comparisons, are published.

Programme dates exclude camping and sales windows. This is the complete available festival information. No additional announcements, social accounts, coordinates, ticket categories, prices or ticket shop are published here.
