### Airport Transfers

To Geneva

Geneva is the most well connected airport for Rise, serving both international flights and budget airlines to all over the UK. These flights are usually available the earliest in the year, so are more likely to grab a bargain. You will be able to book transfers directly with Rise Festival for Geneva.

You will be able to book Saturday - Saturday return transfers (5th-12th) as well as early arrival (Friday 4th Dec) or late arrival (Sunday 7th Dec). Plus early departure (Friday 11th Dec). Mix and match to make your perfect itinerary!

To Grenoble

This is our closest airport you can fly to, though flights are limited.

You will be able to book Saturday - Saturday return transfers (5th-12th). Mix and match with other airports to make your perfect journey. 

To Lyon

Lyon is a little further away, but only by 45 minutes and is more connected.

You will be able to book Saturday - Saturday return transfers (5th-12th). Mix and match with other airports to make your perfect journey. 

How do transfers work?

To book your transfer, you’ll need your flight and accommodation details to hand. Simply purchase your transfer via our website and enter your flight information at checkout. Our partners at Alpine Fleet will then create a schedule based on your flight times and communicate your transfer details directly with you. 

What time will my transfer be?

Allocations of departure time from the airport are made based on your flight arrival time, aiming for no more than 2–3 hours after you land. For departing transfers, your pick-up time is calculated to ensure you have enough time to check in and clear security, typically 4 hours before your flight. 

You’ll receive your allocated times directly from Alpine Fleet via text message 7 days before travel, followed by a confirmation message from GTG 1 day before travel via WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

How long is the wait?

Waits are normally between 2-3 hours after landing, allowing time for border control, baggage collection, and a short refresh stop before boarding your coach.

Where should I go at the airport?

Look out for the Rise x Alpine Fleet stand in the airport arrivals area. Our transfer reps will check you in and direct you to your transfer.

  

Geneva: All transfers depart from the Swiss side of the airport.

Which stop should I get off at?

If you have booked a package with Rise, select “I have booked my package through Rise Festival” and we’ll allocate your stop for you.If you booked a ticket only, please select Stop 1, 2, or 3 when booking. You can check out the map on the website to view the exact location in resort, or use the addresses below. If unsure, we recommend Stop 3, as this is the centre of Les Deux Alpes.

Stop 1 - Entrance to Les Deux Alpes (“Place de Mont-de-Lans”)

Packages: Chalet de Marie, Chalet Le Bouton d'Or, Chalet Le Lauzet, Chalet Le Panorama, Chalet Leslie Alpen, Chalet Levanna Occidentale, Chalet Levanna Orientale, Chalet Névé, Les Glaciers Hotel, Nemea Apartments, People Hostel.

Stop 2 - South End (“1 Rue des Vikings”)

Packages: Base Camp Lodge, Chalet l'Atelier, Chalet la Muzelle, Chalet le Loup, Chalet le Ponton, Chalet Le Renard, Chalet le Soleil Levant, Chalet Prestige Lodge, Chalet Sabot de Venus, Chalet Snö Lodge, Chalet Soleil d'Hiver, Clarines Apartments, Hotel des Neiges, Les Flocons Hotel.

Stop 3 - Centre (“Place des 2 Alpes”)

Packages: Brunerie Hotel, Chalet Chambertin, Chalet le Lys, Chalet Le Marmotton, Chalet les Alpages, l'Ours Blanc, Le Sherpa Hotel, Mainstage Hotel, Neige et Soleil, Rise Alpine Apartments.

How long is the journey?

• Geneva: approx. 220 km - around 3.5 hours

• Lyon: approx. 180 km - around 2.5 hours

• Grenoble: approx. 110 km - around 1.5 hours

Journey times may vary depending on traffic and snow conditions.

What happens if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

If your flight is delayed, Alpine Fleet will do their best to get you onto the next available transfer. If your flight is cancelled, you will need to rebook your transfer. We strongly recommend having travel insurance to cover these costs.

### By Train

Tips for booking trains

If you’re looking for a sustainable travel option, getting the train to Rise will be your best bet. Train travel can be expensive, so to keep your costs down look at these tips below. 

- Book your ticket in advance to get the best price possible 
- Return tickets end up to be cheaper than two separately booked singles 
- Sometimes you can get a Standard Premier seat for just a few pounds more than Standard class.
- Look at using an InterRail pass for discounts 
- Check for group and age related savings

From UK with Eurostar

Get the Eurostar from London to Paris. From Paris get a high-speed TGV train to Grenoble. From there you can work with our transfer operator, for a cost effective transfer to Les 2 Alpes.

### By Car

Road trip to Les 2 Alpes

If you’re looking to make the most of your journey to Rise, then consider a road trip. Once you’ve crossed the channel, it’s a picturesque drive through France right to the Alps. Total journey time is just over 12 hours from London, but we recommend breaking up your pilgrimage into shorter stints to get the most out of your surroundings.

Car rental

If you’re considering a car hire amongst your group from Lyon, Grenoble or Geneva to Les 2 Alpes, there are plenty of rental companies to choose from. Make sure you do your research to find the best price. 

Tips for carpool

Whether you’re looking for a ride or have a free car space, use our Facebook group to connect with other travelers and complete your road trip crew. 

[GO TO FACEBOOK GROUP](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1647484305336103/)

Where to park

Parking in various streets is subject to a subscription - by the hour, day or week and the rate varies according to the zone you're parking in. 

Parking is charged from 08:00 until 19:00, from 1 December to 30 April and 15 June to 31 August.

The two pricing zones are the Central Resort Zone and the Peripheral Zone. Plus, there are three residential zones: 1, 2, and 3.
