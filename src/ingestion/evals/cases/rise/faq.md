### General

When and Where is the festival held?

The incredible French ski resort of Les Deux Alps. Home of Europe's largest skiable glacier from the 5th -12th December 2026.

When should I arrive?

We recommend arriving on the 5th and leaving on the 12th.

Check in, depending on the apartment is usually around 3-5pm.

Is Rise Festival 18+?

Yes.

ID WILL BE REQUIRED TO EXCHANGE FOR A WRISTBAND. WE STRONGLY RECOMMEND NOT RISKING TRAVELLING IF UNDER AGE.

Does the festival take card?

Rise Festival now operates cashless wirstband payments at all of our main stages, the Rise arena and Pano après. Local bars and restaurants accept card. 

Read more about cashless [<u>here</u>](https://www.rise-festival.com/wristband-credit).

What is the line-up?

Phase one lineup is here! Phase two coming soon

[Phase 1 2026 Lineup](https://www.rise-festival.com/line-up)

What's the itinerary?

Wondering what your week at Rise Festival 2026 will look like? We'll be releasing more information very soon.

Can I perform at the festival?

If you are interested in performing at the festival please get in touch via email at hello@rise-festival.com with a link to a mix/social profile etc explaining why you would be suited with Rise.

I'm disabled and thinking of coming to Rise. What are my options?

A free ticket can be issued for a PA to anyone with a disability who purchases a ticket. We just require some evidence (such as DLA or PIP) once the ticket is bought and then we will add the complimentary ticket on. 

Due to core costs of the lift pass, the free PA ticket does not include a lift pass only the festival ticket.

Please find some more information around the resorts accessibility here: 

https://www.les2alpes.com/winter/adapted-tourism/

Please email hello@rise-festival.com  if you need anymore information before booking. 

If you are booking a package with us (i.e. including accommodation), please do let us know, so we can check with the residence on the access, as this varies widely between the accommodations.

How can I contact Rise Festival for any other questions?

hello@rise-festival.com

How can I view/manage my booking?

[**<u>CLICK HERE TO MANAGE/VIEW YOUR RISE FESTIVAL BOOKING </u>**](https://www.rise-festival.com/bookings)

Can I buy a day ticket?

For the best value for money, we recommend coming for a full week and which includes the 6 day lift pass. If you can't for some reason make the full week, we will sell day tickets for the music only without a lift pass. These will be available in late 2026.

### Payment

How can I pay the remaining balance on my booking?

All customers can manage and view bookings [ here](https://www.rise-festival.com/manage-bookings)

Contact us at hello@rise-festival.com if you need any assistance. 

What date is my balance due?

If you have paid by deposit the balance due date will be on your account when you log in. 

If you are paying via payment plan, your instalment is taken on the same date every month. 

Can I pay monthly?

Yes you can, simply choose a payment plan at checkout for the amount to be debited from your account monthly. Alternatively, you may choose to purchase with a deposit. You may then log in and pay any amount each month. Some people choose to log in each month and pay an amount from their balance, others pay everything upfront. It’s completely up to you. 

Can I use Klarna when making a purchase?

If you don't have the funds right now, Rise can now be purchased through Klarna. There are a few ways of doing this... 

  

**PAY IN 3:**

Split your purchases into 3 interest-free monthly payments. The first payment is made at the point of purchase, with the remaining payments scheduled automatically every 30 days. 

**PAY IN 30:  **

Pay nothing when securing your trip in the mountains, and pay the full total fee after 30 days.

  

If there are any issues with making a payment then they can work with you directly to manage the payment terms.  

### Bookings & Tickets

Can I book my ticket then come back at a later date to arrange accommodation and transfers?

Yes! if you can’t find what you are looking for, we recommend purchasing a ticket only option and signing up to our waitlist for accommodation when it's released.

Priority emails of stock are sent to existing customers who have a package or tickets for Rise 2026. 

How do I add guest details to my booking?

Log into your booking on your Rise account [here](https://www.rise-festival.com/manage-bookings) and invite all your guests to add their details. 

Check in is locked and cannot be amended from 4 weeks to the festival so please do it by then! 

Do I need to print my tickets?

No, have your e-tickets ready to scan on your phone.

When will I get my ticket?

E-tickets, festival guides and any important info get sent to ticket holders two weeks before the festival.

I have not received my e-tickets what should I do?

Check your junk, alternatively you can also download these by logging into your account [here](https://www.rise-festival.com/manage-bookings). Please note, if your balance is still unpaid or you have not added all your guests details, you will not have received these. 

I can’t come anymore, can I transfer my booking to someone else?

You can change the name of the booking and therefore transfer your booking over to someone else.

To do this please email us on hello@rise-festival.com

Please note, you will only be able to transfer the whole booking over to someone new, partial booking transfers are not allowed. Additionally, bookings can only be change with the explicit permission from the person who’s name the booking is under.

How do I access a PDF ticket?

To access a PDF version of your ticket please log into your booking online [here](https://www.rise-festival.com/manage-bookings)

Please ensure that all payments on your booking are complete and all guest details are checked in. Only then will the PDF ticket be available to download. 

### Accommodation and Packages

How can I find out the location of my Rise Alpine apartment?

Rise Alpine apartments are a selection of hand picked apartments from various buildings in the resort in the centre. They aren’t one exact location as they are in various buildings across the resort. Saying this, they are all located primely within walking distance of the slopes, bars and stages.

Within 3 weeks of the Festival you will be notified of the name of the building you have been allocated to. If you have friends who have booked under a different name do let us know and we will make an effort to ensure the apartments are near each other.

For more information have a look at the product page [**HERE**](https://www.rise-festival.com/products/rise-alpine-apartments-2026) 

What time can I check in to my accommodation?

Depending on where you have booked, check-in on average between 3pm-5pm on the 5th. 

Rise will notify you of the specific time and location of your accommodation check-in, if you have booked through us.

Where should I stay?

All our packages include accomodation.

If you book via an external provider, feel free to send us the location to verify it is in a convenient location. 

Can I change my package?

You can upgrade your package or add more guests at any time, providing the stock is available. Send us an email to find out on hello@rise-festival.com

Where can I find my accommodation address?

If you go to our  [**Festival Packages**](https://www.rise-festival.com/book-package-2026) & choose the package size that you booked. You'll see the list of accommodation options.   
  
From here you can click **'view full listing' **on your selected accommodation for more information. Scroll further down the page for the address!

Do I need to bring my own linen?

French Alpine Apartments do provide a type of linen, but it is incredibly basic and will be disposable. E.g thin duvets, pillows that have some sort of cover with a bed sheet but aren't your usual standard of hotel linen.

This is why we recommend guests bring their own linen if you would like a better standard of linen on your bed, but this is not compulsory.

### Ski & Board

Where can I find the piste map?

Our home Les 2 Alpes has runs for all abilities. Check out the [<u>L2A website for the latest piste map</u>](https://www.les2alpes.com/winter/discover/skiing-in-les-2-alpes/ski-area/sectors-and-runs/).

Can I add rescue insurance to my lift pass?

Yes! Click the link below to add rescue insurance to your lift pass.

[Add Rescue Insurance](https://assuranceski.com/)

When is the lift pass valid for?

The lift pass is active from the Sunday 6th Dec in the morning until the last lifts on the Friday 11th Dec. You can collect this on Saturday 5th, so you are ready to go for the first lifts on Sunday! If you want a lift pass outside of these times we recommend buying it once you are in resort just for the day.

When is ski and snowboard hire available?

You can buy ski and snowboard hire on our website. 

[BUY HERE](https://www.rise-festival.com/rf26-extras#equipment-hire)

Will ski and snowboard lessons be on sale?

Ski and Snowboard lessons are available to book on our website. These are the options:

1\. Absolute Beginner 12.5 Hour Course - the clue is in the name! 

2\. Beginner Short Course 7.5  Hours - a great way for those who need some support to move up to steeper slopes, picking up speed and practising turns. 

3\. Intermediate Refresher Afternoon 2.5 Hours - An afternoon guided by an instructor to ensure you feel confident for the rest of the week.

[BUY HERE](https://www.rise-festival.com/rf26-extras#lessons)

When and where do I pick up my equipment hire from?

You will be notified of which hire shop you have been allocated to before the festival. Where possible Rise will always try to allocate you to the nearest shop to your accommodation (if you have booked through Rise). Pick up your hire on the afternoon of the 5th Dec, ready for the first lift on Sunday morning! Return your hire by Friday 11th.

How does the waiver for equipment hire work?

The hire equipment waiver is sold through a third party called Pre Fit. They are the best in the business for negotiating claims with shops and ensuring if you do need to make a claim, it is as fair as possible. The excess for the equipment is: standard hire: £200 and premium is £230. Poles are not included. Full Terms are in our Ts and Cs!

What hire level is best for me?

**Standard**

Easy to handle skis, to have a really good time on all slopes of the resort. Perfect for beginners to intermediate level skiers, the skis from the Standard range are very easy to manage. They will allow you to keep on getting better, with peace of mind, on all groomed slopes. With this equipment, making it easy to turn, you will spare yourself and ski effortlessly.

  

**Premium**

Technical and efficient skis, for skiers looking for as much sensations as possible. Highly recommended for great skiers and for intermediate level skiers who want to improve their technique. You will like their accurate manageability in tight bends as well as in wide curves.

### Travel & Arrival

When is the best time to book flights?

We would advise booking flights as soon as you can for the best deals. A lot of the flights by budgets airline may not be out yet. 

We would recommend looking on SkyScanner to find the best priced flights for you and setting alerts for the route you want.

What airports can I fly into?

Geneva is the most well connected airport for Rise, serving both international flights and budget airlines from all over the UK. These flights are usually available the earliest in the year, so are more likely to grab a bargain. Lyon and Grenoble airports are closer but the flights are more limited.

Can I drive?

Yes! There is plenty of parking in the resort too. We recommend pre booking parking and also carefully checking the route and weather conditions before travelling. The mountains can be dangerous, make sure your car is well equipped with snow chains and emergency kit. You can find extensive advice on this on websites such as the RAC.

Here are some useful links if you are considering driving: 

Parking: https://www.see2alpes.com/parking

Driving in the snow: https://www.rac.co.uk/drive/advice/winter-driving/

Ferries: https://www.poferries.com/en/routes/dover-to-calais

Euro Tunnel: https://www.eurotunnel.com/uk/tickets-and-fares/

How can I book transfers?

There are plenty of options when it comes to booking transfers. For Geneva, Grenoble and Lyon you can book these directly with Rise Festival. 

[BUY HERE](https://www.rise-festival.com/rf26-extras)

Where should I head to when I arrive in the resort?

Your first point of call is the Rise Arrivals- located in the main town square by the Jandri lift- use the address 5 Place des 2 Alpes, 38860, to find it!

Can we arrive without the lead booker?

Each person can collect their festival wristband with their e ticket, however the lead booker may only sign out the lift passes. We hope you can understand our strict policy on this.

What if my flights have changed?

You just need to log in to your account, access your booking. Click on 'edit' details and add in the new info here. You can also reach out to us via email hello@rise-festival.com

### Transfers

How do transfers work?

To book your transfer, you’ll need your flight and accommodation details to hand. Simply purchase your transfer via our website and enter your flight information at checkout. Our partners at Alpine Fleet will then create a schedule based on your flight times and communicate your transfer details directly with you. 

What information will I need to book?

You’ll need to provide:  
• Full name  
• Phone number (with country code)  
• Arrival flight date, time, and airline  
• Departure flight date, time, and airline  
• Accommodation details (or your Rise package type)  
  
If you booked accommodation with Rise, we’ll automatically allocate your stop. If you didn’t, please select your stop manually when booking. If you’re travelling by train or arriving on a different date, please make sure to include that information so we can match your details correctly.

What time will my transfer be?

Allocations of departure time from the airport are made based on your flight arrival time, aiming for no more than 2–3 hours after you land. For departing transfers, your pick-up time is calculated to ensure you have enough time to check in and clear security, typically 4 hours before your flight. 

You’ll receive your allocated times directly from Alpine Fleet via text message, followed by a confirmation message from GTG the day before travel via WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

How long is the wait?

Waits are normally between 2–3 hours after landing, allowing time for border control, baggage collection, and a short refresh stop before boarding your coach.

Where should I go at the airport?

Look out for the Rise x Alpine Fleet stand in the airport arrivals area. Our transfer reps will check you in and direct you to your transfer.  
Geneva: All transfers depart from the Swiss side of the airport.

Which stop should I get off at?

If you have booked a package with Rise, select “I have booked my package through Rise Festival” and we’ll allocate your stop for you.If you booked a ticket only, please select Stop 1, 2, or 3 when booking. You can check out the map on the website to view the exact location in resort, or use the addresses below. If unsure, we recommend Stop 3, as this is the centre of Les Deux Alpes.

Stop 1 – Entrance to Les Deux Alpes (“Place de Mont-de-Lans”)Packages: Chalet de Marie, Chalet Le Bouton d'Or, Chalet Le Lauzet, Chalet Le Panorama, Chalet Leslie Alpen, Chalet Levanna Occidentale, Chalet Levanna Orientale, Chalet Névé, Les Glaciers Hotel, Nemea Apartments, People Hostel.

Stop 2 – South End (“1 Rue des Vikings”)Packages: Base Camp Lodge, Chalet l'Atelier, Chalet la Muzelle, Chalet le Loup, Chalet le Ponton, Chalet Le Renard, Chalet le Soleil Levant, Chalet Prestige Lodge, Chalet Sabot de Venus, Chalet Snö Lodge, Chalet Soleil d'Hiver, Clarines Apartments, Hotel des Neiges, Les Flocons Hotel.

Stop 3 – Centre (“Place des 2 Alpes”)Packages: Brunerie Hotel, Chalet Chambertin, Chalet le Lys, Chalet Le Marmotton, Chalet les Alpages, l'Ours Blanc, Le Sherpa Hotel, Mainstage Hotel, Neige et Soleil, Rise Alpine Apartments.

How long is the journey?

• Geneva: approx. 220 km — around 3.5 hours  
• Lyon: approx. 180 km — around 2.5 hours  
• Grenoble: approx. 110 km — around 1.5 hours

Journey times may vary depending on traffic and snow conditions.

What times (Saturday 5th and Saturday 12th) do transfers run?

Arrival Saturday 5th December:

• Geneva: First bus approx. 09:00 | Last bus approx. 16:00 - 21:30  
• Lyon: First bus approx. 10:00 | Last bus approx. 18:30 - 21:30  
• Grenoble: First bus approx. 10:00 | Last bus approx. 16:00 - 21:30  
If your flight arrives later, please book a private transfer.

  

Departures: Saturday 12th December:  
Transfers will only accommodate flights departing after:  
Geneva: 09:30,  Lyon: 09:30, Grenoble: 08:30

  

Saturday Main Departures:  
• Geneva: First departure approx. 02:00 | Last bus approx. 13:00

• Lyon: First departure approx. 03:00 | Last bus approx. 13:00

• Grenoble: First departure approx. 04:00 | Last bus approx. 14:00

Are there other airports to choose from?

Other airports such as Paris, Chambéry, Turin, Marseille, and Nice are possible but not through Rise.

 You may need to book a private transfer or hire a car.

What are my options if I’m flying midweek?

Transfers are not grouped outside of the main travel days (Friday, Saturday, and Sunday). For weekday arrivals or departures, we recommend booking a private transfer via Alpine Fleet or South West Transfers.

What happens if my flight is delayed or cancelled?

If your flight is delayed, Alpine Fleet will do their best to get you onto the next available transfer. If your flight is cancelled, you will need to rebook your transfer. We strongly recommend having travel insurance to cover these costs.

When will I receive my E-ticket and Information?

Your e-ticket will be sent automatically 7 days before your travel day. You will also receive a text message the day before confirming your travel. If you don’t receive a message, contact directly at +41 227 232 984.
