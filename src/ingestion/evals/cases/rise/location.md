# Welcome to Les 2 Alpes

LARGEST SKIABLE GLACIER IN EUROPE • 220KM OF AMAZING PISTES • 2.3KM VERTICAL DROP

Enjoy the second-oldest ski resort in France, the picturesque mountain village of Les Deux Alpes has a rich heritage and is home to Europe’s largest skiable glacier

## Explore the *Resort*

Get to know your new home for the week. Work out where you’re staying, where you’re eating and where you’re skiing. Get ready for a December you won’t forget in a hurry.

### A Snowsports Paradise

Boasting 220km of amazing pistes, the largest skiable glacier, world-renowned beginner slopes and home to the most extensive & award-winning snow park on the continent, Les 2 Alpes is one of the premier ski & snowboard destinations on the planet. 

[EXPLORE SKI & BOARD](https://www.rise-festival.com/snow-resort)

[VIEW PISTE MAP](https://woody.cloudly.space/app/uploads/les-deux-alpes/2021/11/plan-des-pistes-domaine-les-2-alpes-2022.jpg)

### Winter Adventures

There’s more to Rise than great snow, music and a delicious food and drink scene. Our mountain is home to plenty of adrenaline fuelled activities like paragliding, mountain biking and helicopter rides. 

[Explore what’s on](https://www.rise-festival.com/adventure)

Après-Ski Parties

Après is a quintessential element to your ski holiday. You’ve heard about it, but what is it? It's a party on the slopes with your best friends after your best day out on the mountain. Nobody does it better than Les Deux Alpes and Rise Festival. 

Village Vibes

Enjoy a truly magical alpine experience. Les Deux Alpes is both easy to get to and easy to get around. Wherever you stay, you’re never far from the boulangeries, bars, restaurants and shops - so whether you’re on the slopes or off them, there’s something for everyone. 

Alpine Cuisine

Throw yourself head first into mountain culture by sampling the local eateries. Get stuck into fondue or raclettes, finishing every meal with a traditional Génépi, grabbing a warm croissant on your way to the lifts or even sitting down for an indulgent Michelin Star experience.

## Where to *Stay*

There are a huge range of accommodation options. From self-catered apartments to luxury chalets, Les 2 Alpes has a lot to offer. Everything is centrally located and close to the action, so all you have to do is pick the one that’s right for you. Get to know our stays below.

04 CHALETS

Chalets are great for large groups looking to elevate their Rise experience. They sleep up to 16 people and often come with swimming pools, saunas and private terraces. Our chalet selection sells out every year, so get the squad together now. 

01 APARTMENTS

Our apartments prove themselves to be the most popular accommodation option every year. All apartments booked directly with Rise are centrally located and within walking distance to both the lifts and the festival stages. They sleep between two and ten people and come with a kitchenette so you can cook all your meals at home. 

02 HOTELS

If you’re a couple or a group of friends looking for an upgraded experience, this is the option for you. Les 2 Alpes has a selection of higher-end hotels available to book, ranging from half board (meaning breakfast and dinner are included) to breakfast-only options so you can tailor your stay as you like. 

03 HOSTELS
