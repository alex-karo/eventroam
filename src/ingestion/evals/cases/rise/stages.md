# Stages & Parties

The main events and the heart of Rise. Three main stages, an indoor arena and more après scenes than you’re going to know what to do with. 

## Main *Stages*

Rise is hosted across three main stages, five indoor venues and one transformed warehouse afterparty space.

Alpine Stage

Set in the heart of the town, Alpine Stage comes alive each night as we host some of the biggest names in music. Headline acts + heavy snow = an experience unlike anything else.

High Altitude Arena

Known as Pano Bar and set at 2600m the 'High Altitude Arena' hosts everything from headliners to our world famous Rise Festival Aprés shows. 

Muzelle Stage

Positioned at the foot of the slopes, this snowy dancefloor is the perfect spot to take you from après to the early hours of the morning. 

Rise Indoor Arena

We have carefully selected some of electronic music’s biggest hitters to join us at Rise's Indoor Arena - the official home of the Rise Afterparty - so expect a sound clash of epic proportions.

## Around The Town

The party doesn't stop at our stages - whether you're looking for a quiet drink to unwind, or a spot to dance 'til dawn, Les Deux Alpes has you covered.

01/04

### Jukeboxx

Expect a warm welcome as you enter the newly renovated music lovers paradise. As the name suggests, JUKEBOXX boasts an eclectic mix of music. From renowned DJ's to live bands, there’s something for every taste.

### L'Avalanche

Avalanche is the after-afterparty and has earned its international reputation as one of the best equipped, most atmospheric high altitude clubs around. Fitted with state of the art lights and sound, the multi-level layout hosts everything from emerging artists to secret sets. You’ll never know who is going on that night but it’s always one of the best.

### Smithy’s

One of the most popular places to start the party each day. Expect music from drum & bass to house, to hip hop, to disco and everything in between. Our range of world class selectors and special guests always blow the roof off this quaint restaurant turned club house.

### Après Après at Umbrella

Ski straight down a sweet series of blues to one of the most electric après-ski bars in the resort featuring a mini outdoor stage and sound system.
