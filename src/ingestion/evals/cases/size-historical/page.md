# River Festival

Official open-air music festival, Portugal. Public programme: October 1–3, 2026 at River Grounds, scheduled.

The 2025 autumn edition had approximately 7,500 participants. The organizer expects a similar scale for the 2026 autumn weekend with the same programme and grounds. No current attendance total is published.

This is the complete available festival information. No additional announcements, social accounts, coordinates, ticket categories, prices or ticket shop are published here.
