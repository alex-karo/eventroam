# River Festival

Official open-air music festival, Portugal. Two distinct Editions at River Grounds, each with its own programme. Numbers from one Edition do not establish size for the other.

- Participants Edition: October 1–3, 2026. The organizer estimates about 8,000 participants. Venue maximum occupancy is 20,000 people. This Edition is announced and scheduled.
- Repeat-admissions Edition: September 20–22, 2026. Its programme is complete. Venue maximum occupancy is 20,000. Gate counters recorded 24,000 daily admissions over three days, including repeat entries. No unique participant estimate or relevant historical attendance is published.

Programme dates exclude camping and sales windows. This is the complete available festival information. No additional announcements, social accounts, coordinates, ticket categories, prices or ticket shop are published here.
