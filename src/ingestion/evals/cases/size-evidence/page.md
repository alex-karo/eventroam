# River Festival

Official open-air music festival, Portugal. Four distinct programme weekends at River Grounds, all scheduled. These edition names are the organizer's identifiers. Each has a different programme and participant scale; numbers from one weekend do not establish size for another.

- Participants weekend: October 1–3, 2026. The organizer estimates about 8,000 participants. Venue maximum occupancy is 20,000 people.
- Historical weekend: October 10–12, 2026. Its 2025 counterpart had approximately 7,500 participants. The organizer expects a similar scale for this 2026 weekend with the same programme and grounds. No current attendance total is published.
- Repeat-admissions weekend: September 20–22, 2026. Venue maximum occupancy is 20,000. Gate counters recorded 24,000 daily admissions over three days, including repeat entries. No unique participant estimate or relevant historical attendance is published.
- Unknown weekend: November 1–3, 2026. No participant or attendance estimates, including historical comparisons, are published.

Programme dates exclude camping and sales windows. This is the complete available festival information. No additional announcements, social accounts, coordinates, ticket categories, prices or ticket shop are published here.
