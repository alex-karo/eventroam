# Worthy Causes

## Supported charities

Glastonbury supports humanitarian, environmental and community projects. The organisations below have their own campaigns and donation pages.

## Joint Charities — Our Charity Partners

[Oxfam](https://www.oxfam.org.uk/), [WaterAid](https://www.wateraid.org/uk/) and [Greenpeace](https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/) work on poverty, clean water and the environment. Read more about their work in [Joint Charities](https://www.glastonburyfestivals.co.uk/worthy-causes/joint-charities/).

## Other charitable donations

Our support includes medical care, nature protection and local community work. [Médecins Sans Frontières](https://msf.org.uk/) provides medical care in areas of conflict. A £100,000 donation supports its medical work.

Local organisations include [Somerset Wildlife Trust](https://www.somersetwildlife.org/), [Food Forest Project](https://thefoodforestproject.org/) and [Somerset Community Foundation](https://www.somersetcf.org.uk/).

## Volunteering and services

Volunteers support charities through stewarding and other services. [Festival Medical Services](https://festival-medical.org/) is a volunteer-run charity that supports health projects.
