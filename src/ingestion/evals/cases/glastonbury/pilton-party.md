# Pilton Party 2025 in photos

## 7th September 2025

Here are our photos from Friday’s Pilton Party at Worthy Farm, where brilliant performances from RAYE, Inhaler, Revelation Roots and The Outlaw Orchestra helped raise money for Pilton village funds. Thank you to everyone who came along to what was a really great evening on the farm!
