# Proposal

## Why

Visitors need a way to find a similar festival when the dates or format do not suit them.

## What Changes

- Show similar festivals before the Edition history when the catalog has a match.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `website/festival-details`
  - **Before** The Event page shows festival information and published Edition history.
  - **After** The Event page also shows matching alternatives with links to their Editions.

## Impact

Public festival reads and the Event page.
